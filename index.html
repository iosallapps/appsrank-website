Features

Everything you need to track rankings

Real-Time Rankings

See live App Store charts for any country and category. Top Free, Top Paid, and Top Grossing — all updated from iTunes.

Ranking History

Track how your app moves in the charts over 30 days (7 days in the free version). Spot trends, identify drops, and measure the impact of your updates.

Competitor Analysis

Compare your app with up to 3 competitors side by side. See who's ahead, who's behind, and how rankings shift across markets.

Smart Alerts

Get notified when your app enters the Top 10, moves 5+ positions, or enters or leaves the Top 200. Never miss a ranking change.

Global Discovery

Explore Top 3 apps from every country, filter by category, and discover what's trending in markets you've never checked before.

Privacy First

The apps you track and their history stay on your device. No accounts, no sign-in. Rankings come straight from Apple to your device. The free version shows ads and uses analytics, with your consent where the law requires it.

See who is moving, in any market

Top Movers shows the biggest gainers and fallers over 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, for the country and chart you pick.

Add an app to My Apps and AppsRank records its position each time you open it.
