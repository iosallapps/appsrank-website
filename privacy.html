Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Locally Stored Data: Your tracked apps, ranking snapshots, preferences, and cached chart data are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Data We Do NOT Collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing or search history

2. How We Use Data

All app functionality operates locally on your device. Ranking data is fetched directly from Apple's public iTunes RSS feeds and iTunes Search API. These requests go from your device to Apple's servers — we have no server infrastructure and never see your requests.

3. Third-Party Services

The app communicates only with Apple's public APIs:

  • iTunes RSS Feeds — for chart rankings
  • iTunes Search API — for app search
  • iTunes Lookup API — for app metadata
  • Apple StoreKit — for optional support subscriptions

We do not use analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.), advertising networks, social media integrations, crash reporting tools, or any other third-party tracking.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework with iOS encryption. App icons displayed in the app are loaded directly from Apple's content delivery network and are not stored locally.

5. Subscriptions

Optional support subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect or have access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled through your Apple ID settings.

6. Children's Privacy

AppsRank does not collect any personal data from any user, including children under 13. The app does not require account creation or login.

7. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Deleting the app removes all associated data. We retain no data on any server.

8. Your Rights

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app or using the "Clear Cache" option in Settings. Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at iosallapps@icloud.com.
