Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the ranking data come from?

All ranking data is sourced from Apple's public iTunes RSS feeds and iTunes Search API. Data is fetched live from your device — we have no servers.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. We don't collect any personal information, analytics, or tracking data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → AppsRank → Cancel. Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple.

Why is my app showing "100+"?

The iTunes RSS feed returns the top 100 apps per chart. If your app is ranked below #100, it will show as "100+". This is a limitation of Apple's public data.

How often is ranking data updated?

Chart data is fetched live from Apple each time you load it. Rankings are cached for 1 hour to reduce API calls. Pull to refresh to get the latest data.

Does the app work offline?

Previously loaded charts are cached and viewable offline. New data requires an internet connection.

Is it available on Mac?

Yes. AppsRank works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac with the same purchase.
